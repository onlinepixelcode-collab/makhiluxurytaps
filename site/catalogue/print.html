The Makhi promise

Luxury, made simple.

Warranty

Taps, mixers & shower columns: 2 years against manufacturing defects. Freestanding baths & basins: 2 years. Toilets: 1 year on mechanisms.

Returns

7-day change of mind on unused, uninstalled items in original packaging (15% handling fee). Defects: 6 months under the CPA — report within 48 hours.

Payment

Card, EFT, PayJustNow, Laybuy, cash on delivery and in-store.

Delivery

Nationwide delivery, or collect in Edenvale. Fees and times confirmed for your area.

Prices current at publication (November 2026) and subject to change. Installation by a qualified plumber is required for warranty.
